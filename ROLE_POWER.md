# Role power estimates

Each rating is an approximate **1–10** estimate of a single player's influence under the app's current default rules, not an APK-derived or mathematically proven balance value. It considers reliable information, protection and elimination, frequency and risk, and the difficulty of that role's win condition. Pack coordination, player skill, lineup, number of players and optional settings can change the effective strength considerably. Automatic distribution targets average Villager power of 8.5, 5, or 2.5 for Easy, Balanced, or Hard, respectively, and average Werewolf power of 7, 8, or 9. It chooses the closest achievable scores when role chances or Madmen limit the available roles. It adds no Neutral for 4 players, one for 5–11, two for 12–19, and three for 20–24. Neutral roles target an average of 5 points in every mode. These averages are a heuristic, not measured win rates, and independent Neutral win conditions can alter the outcome. Ratings do not change gameplay or update with settings; Random cards have no fixed rating because the role is not yet known.

## Villagers

| Villager | 1 | Ordinary vote and discussion, no night power; baseline. |
| Muted | 1 | Ordinary vote with a communication restriction and no night ability. |

| Sick Man | 2 | Standard village participation but a survival deadline without Doctor visits. |
| Journalist | 3 | Public communication tool; its effect depends on the information and players. |
| Mayor | 7 | One emergency assembly protects the village and grants lasting double votes. |
| Doctor | 8 | Repeated protection can prevent key deaths, with repeat-target restrictions. |
| Seer | 9 | Nightly role information strongly directs village voting, despite disguises. |
| Chef | 7 | Recurring two-player team comparison; informative but indirect. |
| Templar | 6 | One powerful wolf elimination, with a lethal penalty for a mistaken target. |
| Necromancer | 8 | One revival can restore a valuable role or reverse an important death. |
| Bodyguard | 6 | Repeatedly protects villagers but sacrifices themselves to intercept a death. |
| Sheriff | 6 | One unrestricted shot can remove a threat or accidentally kill an innocent. |
| Visitor | 2 | No direct information or offensive benefit in the implemented rules, with substantial risk. |
| Psychic | 8 | Nightly team information, less specific than a Seer's exact role reading. |
| Detective | 6 | Repeated visit reports can reveal actions indirectly, and arrive on the next turn. |
| Instigator | 6 | Twice can add two votes to a target, affecting a vote without guaranteeing it. |
| Intelligence Chief | 5 | Periodic, potentially inaccurate intelligence provides limited guidance. |
| Police | 8 | Repeated silent action blocking can disrupt strong abilities without alerting targets. |

| Role | Power | Reasoning |
| --- | ---: | --- |

| Wolf Pup | 8 | Pack voting plus a revenge boost to the next pack attack after death. |
